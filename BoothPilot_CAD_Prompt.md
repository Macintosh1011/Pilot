# BoothPilot — 3D-Printed Booth Enclosure: CAD prompt (simple)

> Paste into your CAD tool. Houses only the iPad.

---

Design a 3D-printable tabletop stand/enclosure that holds an **iPad Pro 12.9″ (5th gen)** in **landscape**, tilted back ~12° for a standing viewer. Clean and minimal with rounded edges and a wide, stable base.

- **iPad pocket:** 281 × 215 × 7 mm (the iPad is 280.6 × 214.9 × 6.4 mm) with a ~6 mm front lip; iPad is removable.
- Keep one short edge **clear for the front camera**; leave openings for the speakers and a **charging cable** (routed out the back).
- No other parts inside — just the iPad and its charge cable.
- **Colors:** bone-white body, one accent in gray or cyan, tiny pink details only.
- **Print:** PLA, ~3 mm walls. The frame is ~281 mm wide, so **split it into parts** that fit a 256 mm bed and join with screws/pins.

Output a parametric model + per-part STLs.
